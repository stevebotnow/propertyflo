Company

PropertyFlo, a better property transaction for everyone.

One of life’s bigger challenges is navigating a property transaction. We built PropertyFlo to make this easier for everyone—providing one central platform to communicate and exchange essential sensitive information.

Property transactions are broken. Too many emails. Too many platforms. Too many parties. PropertyFlo fixes this by uniting the entire transaction in one central place.

Our goal is to turn a challenging experience into one that’s simple, smooth, and painless.

The founder

Scott O'Brien is the founder of PropertyFlo. He brings together a lifelong passion for real estate with over a decade of experience building enterprise software. Before launching PropertyFlo, Scott spent over eight years as an engineer at ServiceNow, where he specialized in designing workflows and solving complex business challenges through technology. Today, he applies that same expertise to creating tools that simplify property transactions and make the process easier, clearer, and more efficient for everyone involved.
